# Email-to-Quote AI Agent

## Project Summary

Email-to-Quote AI Agent is an agentic AI prototype being developed for Sage Publishing to automate the initial stages of customer quotation processing.

The system monitors Outlook emails, extracts information from both the email body and attachments, validates customer and product information against Business Central (currently mocked), generates a professional quotation PDF, routes the quote through a mandatory human approval step, and finally prepares a customer-facing response.

The project is designed as an enterprise workflow rather than a simple LLM application. AI assists with understanding documents and drafting responses, while humans remain responsible for approving commercial communications.

---

## Business Problem

Sales teams spend significant time manually:

- Reading quotation requests
- Opening attachments
- Extracting requested products
- Validating customers
- Looking up products
- Preparing quotation documents
- Writing response emails

Many of these activities are repetitive and rule-based.

The objective is to reduce manual effort while ensuring that all customer-facing communications remain under human control.

---

## Objectives

Primary objectives:

- Automate quotation request processing
- Read Outlook emails automatically
- Process multiple attachment formats
- Extract structured business information using an LLM
- Validate against Business Central
- Generate professional quotations
- Require human approval before customer communication
- Prepare Outlook draft responses

---

## Guiding Principles

- Human-in-the-loop architecture
- AI assists but does not make commercial decisions
- Modular agent-based design
- Easy replacement of mock services with production APIs
- Prompt-driven behaviour instead of hardcoded logic
