# Known Limitations

## Current Prototype

The system currently uses:

- Outlook COM
- Mock Business Central
- Local file storage
- Local OCR

This is intentional.

---

## Business Central

Customer validation is mocked.

Future implementation should replace mock_bc.py with Business Central APIs.

---

## Outlook

Uses Outlook COM.

Chosen because Microsoft Graph authentication was blocked by enterprise Token Protection policies.

Future production version may migrate back to Graph.

---

## Quote Generation

Current pricing is static.

Future version should retrieve:

- Customer-specific pricing
- Discounts
- Tax
- Regional pricing

---

## Email Composer

Prompt is still being refined.

Future version should:

- Adapt tone automatically.
- Use customer history.
- Support multiple workflow types.

---

## Human Review

Currently console based.

Future versions should use:

- Streamlit
- Internal Web Portal
- Power Apps

---

## Logging

Current logging is basic.

Future implementation should support:

- Centralized logging
- Audit trails
- Approval history
